02 — Our Story

The town that
bought out the case.

1990

A family, a fryer, and 5 AM

John and Stella Chhan came to California as refugees from Cambodia and opened a small donut shop on Pacific Coast Highway. Every morning since, the lights have come on before dawn — John at the fryer, Stella at the counter, every donut rolled by hand.

2018

The morning Seal Beach showed up

When Stella was recovering from an aneurysm, the neighborhood made a quiet pact: buy the donuts out early — dozens at a time, every single morning — so John could close up and spend the day at her side. The story traveled from Seal Beach to newsrooms around the world.

Today

Stella came back. The line never left.

She recovered. The case still empties early. And the little shop a block from the beach is still proof that a town and a donut shop can love each other back.
