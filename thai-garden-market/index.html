Our Story

The Taste of Thailand, In Your Kitchen

Thai Garden Market was founded in 1998 by the Rattana family, who moved to Anaheim from Bangkok and immediately noticed something missing: you couldn't find real Thai ingredients anywhere in Orange County.

So they fixed that. Starting with a small storefront and a handful of imported products, they built the most trusted Thai grocery in OC over the next 25 years. Today, we carry over 2,000 products — fresh, frozen, dried, and prepared — sourced directly from Thailand and the broader Southeast Asian region.

Whether you're a home cook, a restaurant owner, or just someone craving the real thing, Thai Garden Market is your destination.
