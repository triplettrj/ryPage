Our Story

Honest Work, Fair Prices, Family Values

Chai Wongprasert opened Chai's Auto Care in 2001 with one simple goal: fix cars honestly. After working for years at dealerships where upselling was the norm, he decided to do things differently.

For over 20 years, we've served Fullerton, Anaheim, and Brea with transparent diagnostics, fair prices, and repairs that actually last. We only recommend what you need. We explain everything before we touch your car. And we back every job with our warranty.

From oil changes to engine rebuilds, Chai's team handles it all. Bring us your car — we'll take care of the rest.
