Athletes: your first class

  1. Put on your heart rate strap or armband (most only turn on when they touch skin), or wear your Apple Watch.
  2. iPhone: open the PulseClass app, enter your name and age, and tap Connect monitor (or Use Apple Watch). Android: open this site in Chrome and tap Connect monitor.
  3. Tap Join a class, then Scan QR code on the poster, the TV or your coach's screen. Or type the class code.

Your coach and the room TV see your heart rate, % of max and effort points. Turn on Share with the class to see classmates who also share, and a leaderboard.

Zones and effort points

Your max heart rate is estimated as 220 − your age. Zones are % of that: Zone 1 Warm-up 50–60%, Zone 2 Easy 60–70%, Zone 3 Aerobic 70–80%, Zone 4 Threshold 80–90%, Zone 5 Max 90%+. You earn 1 effort point a minute in Zone 1, 2 in Zone 2, and so on up to 5 a minute in Zone 5.

Heart rate devices

  • Chest straps and armbands with Bluetooth work as soon as you put them on: Polar, Wahoo, Garmin HRM-Dual, HRM-Pro, HRM 600 and HRM-Fit, COROS, Scosche, Suunto, Magene, Coospo, Myzone and others. The nearest one is marked Closest · probably yours.
  • Watches and bands that broadcast heart rate (Garmin, Polar, COROS, Amazfit, Huawei, WHOOP, Google Pixel Watch 2 and 3): turn on the watch's heart rate broadcast setting first. In the iPhone app, Connect monitor › My device isn't listed has the steps for each brand. Some only show up with Show all Bluetooth devices on.
  • Fitbit Charge 6: start an exercise on the tracker, connect to it, and approve the share prompt on the tracker (it asks every time). Fitbit doesn't allow this in Europe.
  • Powerbeats Pro 2 show up as connected to your iPhone. Earbud heart rate can drop out; a strap is more reliable.
  • Gym bikes with Bluetooth can pass on the heart rate of a strap paired to the bike. Connect to the bike.
  • Can't be used: straps that only use ANT+ (iPhones don't have it), Samsung Galaxy Watch, Oura, Fitbits other than the Charge 6, most Suunto watches and Xiaomi bands.

Apple Watch

  1. Check PulseClass is on your Watch. If it isn't, on your iPhone open the Watch app, scroll to Available Apps and tap Install.
  2. In the iPhone app, join a class and tap Use Apple Watch. Or open PulseClass on the Watch and tap Start.
  3. The first time, allow Health access on the Watch.

Your Watch sends heart rate to the class even with your iPhone locked in your bag. Keep the iPhone nearby. Tap End on the Watch after class; the workout saves to Health and counts toward your rings. App updates install on the Watch only after a workout ends.

Coaches

Start a class

Open the coach dashboard and tap Create class. For a studio, tick Permanent room: the code and TV link never change, so you can print a poster once and leave the TV bookmarked.

On the dashboard

  • Sort by name, heart rate, % of max or points.
  • Show join QR shows a big QR code to scan. Print poster makes a sheet for the wall.
  • Open TV mode or Copy TV link for the room screen. Add &sort=points to the TV link to rank by effort points, or &sort=name for alphabetical order. The TV link can't change anything.
  • Class view on phones lets athletes who share see each other. Turn it off for classes that shouldn't compare.
  • Class summary shows everyone's points, average, peak and time in zones, with a CSV download. Download it before you tap New session, which resets everything for the next class.

Keep the dashboard link private: it has a key that controls the class. Anyone with the join code can only join and send heart rate.

Troubleshooting

My strap doesn't show up

Wet the sensors and put it on (most sleep until they touch skin). Make sure it isn't connected to another app or phone, like Strava or a gym bike. Close other fitness apps and try again.

Checking which devices work

In the iPhone app, tap Connect monitor › Test several devices. Put on up to five devices and tap each one: you'll see their heart rates side by side, with any drop-outs. Share results sends a report. Nothing is sent to your class.

"Your strap isn't touching skin"

Wet the electrodes (or your skin under them) and tighten the strap a little.

Connected, but no heart rate

Same as above, for a strap. For a watch, check heart rate broadcast is on. For a Fitbit Charge 6, approve the share prompt on the tracker. For a bike, pair your strap with the bike first.

My Apple Watch isn't found

Install the Watch app from Available Apps (see Apple Watch). If the iPhone says it can't start, unlock your Watch, open PulseClass on it and tap Start.

"Class not found"

Check the code with your coach. Temporary classes are removed after 12 hours without activity; permanent rooms stay.

Android: it stops when the screen turns off

Keep the PulseClass page open and on screen during class; the page keeps the screen awake while a monitor is connected. For a locked phone, use the iPhone app or an Apple Watch.
