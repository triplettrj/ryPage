PulseClass privacy

Last updated November 6, 2026. This covers the PulseClass iPhone and Apple Watch apps, the web app, and the PulseClass server.

The short version

  • Your heart rate goes only to the class you join, while you're in it.
  • Heart rate is never written to disk, sold, or used for ads. There's no tracking, no analytics and no account.
  • Classmates see you only if you turn on Share with the class.

What the apps send, and to whom

When you join a class, the app sends your name, an estimate of your maximum heart rate (from your age, which stays on your phone), your live heart rate and whether you chose to share. It goes only to the PulseClass server of that class, which is usually run by your gym or coach. Your coach's dashboard and the room's TV show your name and heart rate. Other athletes see you only if you both turn on sharing.

Each phone makes a random ID, different for every server, so you keep your place if the connection drops. It isn't linked to your Apple ID or anything else.

What the server keeps

During a session the server holds, in memory only: your name, current heart rate, average and peak, effort points, time in each zone, and a 10-minute trend line. It's deleted when you tap Leave class, when the coach starts a new session, 30 minutes after you disconnect, or when the server restarts. A coach can download a summary of the session (names, points, average and peak, time in zones) from their dashboard.

The only thing written to disk is each class's name, code, access keys and settings. Never heart rate or athlete names.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

The Apple Watch app reads your heart rate during workouts that you start, and saves the workout to Apple Health on your devices, where it counts toward your Activity rings. Health data is used only to show your heart rate in the class you join. It isn't used for advertising, isn't sold, and isn't shared with anyone else.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth: to connect to your heart rate monitor or gym bike.
  • Camera: only to scan a class QR code. Scanning happens on your phone; no image is saved or sent.
  • Local network: to reach a class server on your gym's own network.
  • Health: to read heart rate and save workouts, as above.

Your choices

You can leave a class at any time, turn sharing off, or deny any permission in Settings › PulseClass. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone (your name, age and ID).

Questions

For a class, ask whoever runs that PulseClass server, usually your gym. For the app itself, use the contact details on the PulseClass App Store page.
