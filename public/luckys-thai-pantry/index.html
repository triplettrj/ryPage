Our Story

A Taste of Thailand, Right Here in Orange County

Lucky's Thai Pantry has been feeding Orange County families since 2005. Founded by the Siriporn family, we brought authentic recipes straight from Chiang Mai and Bangkok — and we haven't changed a thing.

Every dish is prepared fresh to order using herbs and spices imported directly from Thailand, alongside locally sourced vegetables. We're more than a restaurant — we're also your neighborhood Thai grocery, so you can recreate those flavors at home.

Whether it's a quick weekday lunch, a family dinner, or a catered event, we put the same love into every plate we serve.
