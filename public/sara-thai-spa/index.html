Our Philosophy

Healing Is an
Ancient Art

Thai massage is not merely relaxation — it is a 2,500-year-old healing system rooted in Buddhist spiritual practice. At Sara Thai Spa, we honor that tradition in every treatment we offer.

Our founder, Sara Wongsawat, trained under master practitioners in Bangkok and Chiang Rai before bringing this refined practice to Irvine in 2008. Every therapist we employ completes the same rigorous training and shares the same philosophy: that true wellness is the harmony of body, mind, and spirit.

This is not a day spa. This is a destination for those who take their wellbeing seriously.
