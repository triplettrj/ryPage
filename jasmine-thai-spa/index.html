Our Story

Ancient Traditions, Modern Comfort

Jasmine Thai Spa was founded in 2010 by Malee Srisuk, a certified Thai massage therapist trained in Chiang Mai's renowned massage schools. She brought the healing art of nuad boran — traditional Thai massage — to the heart of Orange County.

Our therapists average over 10 years of hands-on experience. Every treatment is personalized to your body's needs, whether you're unwinding after a long week or recovering from an active lifestyle.

From the moment you walk through our doors, the stress of the outside world fades away. That's our promise to every guest.
