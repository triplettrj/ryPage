Our Story

Grown Up in a Thai Kitchen

Hungry Boy Thai Cafe was born from a simple idea: serve the food we grew up eating, at prices everyone can afford. Owner Pon Chaisuk started cooking at age 8 alongside his grandmother in Bangkok, and brought those recipes to Westminster in 2012.

No fancy plating, no pretense — just blazing woks, fresh produce, and the same bold flavors you'd find on a Bangkok side street. Every dish is made to order. Every order is made with care.

We've been feeding Westminster, Garden Grove, and Fountain Valley for over 12 years. Come see why the regulars keep coming back three times a week.
